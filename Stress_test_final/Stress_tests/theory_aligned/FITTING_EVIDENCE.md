## Exact metric

**Test NRMSE**, defined in
`Interface_code/DeepDissipative_NLink_main.py::_rmse_nrmse`:

$$
\mathrm{RMSE}=\sqrt{\tfrac1N\sum(\hat y-y)^2},\qquad
\mathrm{NRMSE}=\mathrm{RMSE}/\max(\mathrm{std}(y),10^{-12})
$$

computed **once** over the full test-set array: all 100 test trajectories ×
190 open-loop rollout steps × 2 output channels, flattened together. This is
a single global RMSE normalized by the global std of the true test signal —
**not** a per-trajectory average.
